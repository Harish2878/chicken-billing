OFFICIAL PRIVACY STATEMENT

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Sri Kanaka Durga Chicken Center ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and safeguards information through the SKD Chicken Billing application.

1. Information We Collect

Our application is a dedicated business billing and supply management tool. We collect:

  • Business Supply Records: Dates of delivery, number of chickens supplied, weights in kg, market paper rates, and calculated totals.
  • Account Details: Contact person names (e.g., N Ravi Kumar), phone numbers, and delivery addresses of partner hotels.
  • Authentication Data: Securely hashed passwords and JWT session tokens for role-based authentication (Supplier Admin vs. Hotel View-Only).

2. Use of Information

Information gathered is used exclusively for:

  • Generating daily supply challans and tax invoices.
  • Computing wholesale rate deductions (Standard ₹60/kg deduction).
  • Maintaining credit/debit transaction ledgers and customer pending balances.
  • Formatting daily delivery reports for WhatsApp communication upon explicit user action.

3. Data Protection & Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including bcrypt password hashing, encrypted HTTPS communication, and role-based access control. Novel Hotel accounts have strict view-only access and cannot alter billing data or financial records.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or business data to any third parties. Reports are shared only through direct user-initiated channels (such as WhatsApp sharing).
